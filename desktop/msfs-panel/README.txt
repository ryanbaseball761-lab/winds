Trade Winds in-sim panel
========================
Puts Trade Winds in the MSFS toolbar: your job, checklist, live numbers, cabin feed and sealed orders, without alt-tabbing.

One-time setup
1. Install the free MSFS SDK: in MSFS turn on Developer Mode (Options > General > Developers),
   then in the top menu bar choose Help > SDK Installer. Restart your PC afterwards.
2. Double-click build-and-install.bat. It builds the panel and copies it into your Community folder.
3. Start MSFS. Click the Trade Winds icon in the toolbar at the top of the screen.

The Trade Winds app must be running. The panel shows the app's live state from http://localhost:4747/panel.
