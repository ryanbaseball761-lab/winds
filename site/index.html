The game

Not a job board. A story with choices and endings.

Career add-ons give you cargo. Trade Winds gives you Castillo, a soft-spoken businessman who needs a pilot who doesn't ask questions, and a DEA agent who has started asking them about you.

Twenty chapters

Every chapter is a real flight

Each chapter has a route, an aircraft role and a goal. Fly it in the sim, then live with what you chose. Five acts, several endings and a New Game+ where someone else is the leak.

Live from the sim

It knows how you flew

The flight starts when your engine runs and ends when you park. Mid-flight orders come in: climb away from a shadowing jet, stay under 1,000 feet, take the Coast Guard's call. The landing is graded.

Between chapters

Your own corner of the Caribbean

Side jobs, checkrides, an airline to run, businesses that wash money, a customs officer who can be paid, and Heat that rises every time you push your luck.

Made for streams

Let Twitch chat decide

Turn on chat voting and your viewers pick the story choices by typing 1, 2 or 3. Texts from the cast land on an in-game phone between flights.

Install

Up and running in five minutes.

01

Download

Get TradeWinds-Setup.exe from the latest release and run it.

02

Start MSFS

Load into any airport in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 or 2020.

03

Open Trade Winds

It connects to the sim by itself. Start chapter 1 on the Story page.

"Windows protected your PC"? The beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. The game updates itself after that.

Report from the game

Go to Hangar → Saves & help → Report a problem, describe what happened, then press Open a GitHub issue. Your report is filled in for you. Or open an issue here.

Most useful to us

  • Anything confusing in the story: who someone is, why you're flying somewhere
  • A flight that didn't start, didn't finish, or got graded wrong
  • Money or Heat that feels unfair or stuck

Privacy

Your saves stay on your PC. The app contacts GitHub for game updates and art, Google Fonts, and the US National Hurricane Center for real storms. OpenAI only if you add your own key for premium voices.

Known in this beta

  • The in-sim toolbar panel isn't included yet
  • Briefings follow MSFS live weather (add-ons can't set it)
