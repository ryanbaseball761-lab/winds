# Trade Winds: store page copy

## One line
A crime story you fly yourself: from a borrowed Cessna to a 747 full of contraband, across 110 real Caribbean airports in MSFS 2024.

## Short pitch (about 50 words)
You're a fresh pilot in St. Martin with a cheap job and a cracked seat. Then the mechanic asks for a small favor. Fly the whole rise yourself in Microsoft Flight Simulator: twenty chapters, five acts, hard choices, and one last run into Miami that decides who runs the Caribbean.

## Long description
**It starts with a box.** A ten-minute hop to Anguilla, a taped box that isn't on the manifest, and two hundred dollars in your shirt pocket. Your hands are slick on the yoke.

**It ends with a 747.** Twenty chapters later you're flying eighty tons through the night with the Coast Guard on the radio, the DEA waiting in Miami and the man who made you riding in your jump seat.

Trade Winds is a story career that runs alongside MSFS. Every chapter is a real flight you fly yourself, with cinematic cutscenes before and after, phone calls and radio calls in flight, and a decision at the end that the story remembers.

### Features
- **A 20-chapter crime story in five acts**: Clean Hands, Fast Money, Bigger Wings, Heavy Metal and The Last Run. Cessna to Vision Jet, Citation CJ4, 737 and 747.
- **Choices that come back.** Hide the case or leave it. Pass the oxygen back or keep your eyes forward. Let Tico sit up front. Small choices change later scenes, who stands with you and how it all ends.
- **Three endings**: Kingpin, Witness or Ghost. You only get the ones you earned.
- **Power and Heat.** Every run makes you stronger and more wanted. At Heat 60 customs starts searching your plane; at 80 the feds are watching and you have to lie low.
- **Run your own operation**: dirty cash to launder, crew to hire, safehouses to buy, your own cargo airline, and a fleet that costs money every day.
- **Graded by MSFS.** Trade Winds reads your flight live: it starts the flight when the engine runs, finishes it when you park, and grades your touchdown point, centerline, sink rate, fuel and airmanship.
- **An instructor and a copilot** who call out what matters: unstable approaches, overspeeds, gear, sink rate, fuel.
- **Earn your licences**: 11 ratings with real checkrides, from Private Pilot to Airline Transport Pilot, including the St. Barth and Saba qualifications.
- **Side work between chapters**: a job board across 110 airports, an eight-chapter legit side story flying private jets, sealed-order emergencies, the Butter Cup landing challenge, 14 academy lessons, weekly challenges and 50 achievements.
- **New Game+** when the story's over: keep your licences, start the network again, and Heat rises faster.

## Requirements
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 (MSFS 2020 also works) on Windows.
- The Trade Winds desktop app and its MSFS bridge (included).
- Aircraft: the story uses the default Cessna 172, Cirrus Vision Jet, Citation CJ4, Boeing 737 MAX and Boeing 747-8F. Side jobs use any of 26 supported aircraft you own.
- Optional: an OpenAI key for premium character voices (Windows voices work without it).

## FAQ
**Do I need to fly every chapter by hand?** Yes. That's the point. There's a practice mode and a Casual difficulty if you want it gentler.

**Does it change MSFS?** No. It reads your flight through the bridge and can set fuel, time and your parking spot when you ask it to.

**How long is the story?** Most players finish the main story in 20 to 30 flights, plus whatever side work they choose.

**Is it violent?** It's a crime story: smuggling, bribery, betrayal. Nothing graphic is shown.
