# Trade Winds 0.81: fifty fixes and upgrades

1. The title screen is a still picture (no rain, pan or flashing).
2. "Reduce motion" also calms the newer animations.
3. The same pop-up twice in a row shows once; never more than three at a time.
4. Panels that open over the page keep keyboard focus inside and return it when closed.
5. Phone: "Mark all read".
6. Phone: a count of messages and new ones.
7. Reyes's interview can be reopened from her text.
8. A failure that forces a landing elsewhere counts as a proper diversion.
9. The Coast Guard waits 30 seconds, then you've complied.
10. Daily costs include hull insurance.
11. Sell a property for 60% of the price.
12. Your aircraft's name shows on the boarding pass and the job board.
13. The job board sorts by departure, pay or flight time (remembered).
14. The top bar warns when Castillo's loan is due within three days.
15. This week's event shows on the Story page.
16. The Islands list shows the eight that matter most.
17. Enter on a result screen goes to the next step.
18. M mutes and unmutes everything.
19. The command palette (Ctrl+K) finds the phone, property, islands, aircraft logbooks and mute.
20. Keyboard hints no longer re-run on every page change (smoother).
21. The radar says "No weather data" instead of disappearing.
22. The route strip shows the game-clock time you'll land.
23. Chapter cards wait for the right font.
24. The customs scene plays at most once per airport per day.
25. "Flight details" on the result remembers if you keep it open.
26. The boarding pass shows at most three tags.
27. New stats: texts answered, ATC followed, short fuel caught, welcoming islands, Halcón record.
28. The title screen says when you last played.
29. Credits, from the title menu.
30. Settings: show every system from the start, for players who don't want the gradual introductions.
31. A chapter you can't fly yet says which aircraft you need.
32. "Next" in the top bar tells you when Reyes wants to see you.
33. Night customs scenes happen at night.
34. The bank's weekly text includes what insurance cost.
35. Insured flights say so on the boarding pass.
36. Old per-day markers are cleaned out of the save.
37. Halcón races add your win/loss record to the result.
38. A new weekly event pops up the first time you open the job board.
39. Texts from someone you've upset drop the friendly emoji.
40. Spanish covers the new screens.
41. Texts waiting for an answer sit at the top of the phone.
42. Sealed destinations say "Sealed", never "????".
43. The map shows this week's event.
44. You read ATC instructions back ("Heading 140, Coral Wing 21.").
45. The beta question asks once per chapter, even on replays.
46. The achievements list counts secrets found.
47. Twitch settings explain !tip.
48. Enter in the friend-code box adds it.
49. Properties you can't afford say how much you're short.
50. A sound when a flight order is done or missed, so you hear it inside the sim.

Also: timed choices give you 30 seconds and pause while the game window is hidden (so alt-tabbing into MSFS doesn't cost you the choice), the title fits a phone, and "last played just now" instead of "0 min ago".
