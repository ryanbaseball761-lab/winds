# Story audit (main story, 20 chapters)

The story structure is sound. The five acts escalate cleanly from Cessna to Vision Jet, CJ4, 737 and 747; the rating gates (JET from chapter 5, ATP from chapter 15) are in the right places; every chapter has a prologue, a goal, in-flight beats and an outcome; and all three endings can be reached.

## Choices that never pay off (flag set, never read)

| Chapter | Choice | Flag | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 The Passengers | Pass the oxygen back | `oxygen` | Nothing ever mentions it again. |
| 7 The Files | Copy Arnaud's drive | `drivecopy` | The echo line plays once; the "insurance" is never used. |
| 9 Reyes | Hide the case in the avionics bay | `hidcase` | Reyes's search plays out the same whether you hid it or not. |
| 10 The Citation | Let Tico take the right seat | `ticoSeat` | Never comes back, even when you fly Tico to Grand Cayman two chapters later. |
| 13 Dez | Tell the lawyer everything | `toldLawyer` | Castillo's lawyer hears everything and nobody reacts. |
| 15 Hartwell's Secret | Promise Hartwell a seat out | `hartwellSafe` | Hartwell is never seen again in any ending. |
| 17 The Setup | Go to war / Act like nothing happened | `war`, `patient` | The two choices lead to identical chapters 18 to 20. |

## Missed dramatic moments
- **Chapter 12, The Rat.** If you took Reyes's deal in chapter 9, *you* are the one talking to the DEA. Castillo blames Tico, and the game never notices.
- **Reyes disappears** for chapters 10 to 19 except one text message. An informant hears nothing from her handler for half the story.
- **The epilogue** covers Mari, Dez, Tico and the boy only. Castillo, Reyes, Medina and Hartwell get no ending line.

## Smaller issues
- Chapter 3 recap: "Now you owe him." It's Dez, not Castillo, so it doesn't break your rule, but it's the only time the story frames the player as in debt.
- Chapter 13 and 14 recaps ("You decided what happened to him") are vague. The Story page shows "You chose: …" underneath, which covers it.
- The witness ending only counts the Reyes deal, the ledgers or burning Medina. Copying Arnaud's drive, or quietly writing everything down (the "wait" choice), are evidence too and should count.

## Proposed fixes (Phase 2)
1. Every unused choice pays off at least once: in a later prologue line, a different outcome, or the epilogue.
2. Chapter 12 gets an informant version: you know the real rat, and Tico might be about to die for you.
3. War vs. wait changes chapters 18 to 20: who's with you on the ramp, what Castillo says, what you hold over him.
4. Reyes checks in on informants in Acts 3 and 4 (phone calls and texts).
5. The epilogue gains lines for Castillo, Reyes, Medina and Hartwell, depending on your choices and ending.
6. The witness ending also opens with Arnaud's drive or your notebook.
